PM Morning Address

16 Feb 2027 · 09:00 GMT · Live Broadcast
The Prime Minister

Good morning.

I am speaking to you from Downing Street.

Last night I asked you to stay local, to follow the guidance, and to look after each other. You have done so, and the country has had a calm and orderly night. I want to thank you for that, briefly, before we move on.

Here is where we are this morning.

Overnight there were a small number of isolated incidents which were quickly contained by police and military personnel. Power, water, gas, and emergency services have remained fully operational throughout. There has been no further attack, here or in any of the other affected nations. I have spoken with my counterparts this morning. The picture is consistent. The response is coordinated.

I want to be clearer than I think we have been so far about what is actually offline and why. The infrastructure has been damaged — that is what I told you last night and it remains true. The damage is to the security layer underneath our banking and payment systems. But most of the systems sitting on top of that layer — the apps you use, the card networks, the bank databases — have not been damaged in themselves. They have been paused while the security layer underneath is being rebuilt to a higher standard. Your bank's database has not been emptied. Your records have not been lost. We will turn them back on, in sequence, as quickly as it is responsible to do so.

We have turned those
services off ourselves,
deliberately, as a precaution.

Restoration work has been underway since yesterday morning. The assessment we have completed overnight gives us confidence in the timeline I set out last night — days, not weeks. The Chancellor and the Chief Executive of the National Cyber Security Centre will set out the detail with me at six o'clock this evening, including the schedule for the first systems to come back online tomorrow.

In the meantime — and this is the practical part.

A network of designated pharmacies and food retailers is opening across the country this morning under restricted hours, on a cash-only basis. Please be patient with staff. Please take only what your household needs. Queues are expected.

From midday today, Community Recovery Hubs will open at community centres, libraries, leisure centres, and places of worship in every local authority area. These are not food distribution centres. They are warm, welcoming places — for anyone who needs help, information, a hot meal, or simply somebody to talk to. Particularly if you are alone, or elderly, or finding this period difficult, please use them. Please tell your neighbours about them. Their locations are being distributed through ARC Emergency this morning.

Schools, hospitals for routine appointments, and public transport remain as I set out last night.

Communications networks are running with delays. Telephone calls and text messages are working, but congested — please reserve them for essential contact. Email and messaging applications are intermittent. ARC Emergency and BBC broadcast remain reliable.

Until this evening. Be well.
