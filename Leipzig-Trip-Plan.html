UEFA Conference League Final
Crystal Palace vs Rayo Vallecano

Red Bull Arena, Leipzig · 47,000 capacity · 21:00 CEST
Palace's first-ever European final. Rayo's too. History either way.

Getting to Lagovida after the match

Pre-book your taxi — don't wing it

47,000 people pouring out = taxi chaos. Best options:

FreeNow or Bolt app — pre-book a pickup from Leipzig Hauptbahnhof (tram there first, runs late on match nights)
Taxi Leipzig: +49 341 4884 — call to pre-arrange a time/location
LeipzigMOVE app — official city app with taxi integration
Private transfer: GetTransfer.com or AirportTransfer.com

If one of you isn't drinking: P+R Völkerschlachtdenkmal is on the route south to Großpösna — you could drive yourselves.

Overnight

Lagovida — Das Ferienresort am Störmthaler See ↗

Lakeside resort in Großpösna, 20 min south of Leipzig. Lake views, restaurant, bar, private beach, sauna. A peaceful contrast to the match-night madness.
