Sixty-one per cent of card transactions are now processing successfully. Some regions are above seventy per cent. The numbers speak for themselves.

Markets & bankingThe London Stock Exchange is scheduled to reopen next week, subject to verification of settlement infrastructure. Designated bank branches opened for cash withdrawals as planned.

BankingMany of you will have logged in to your banking applications today for the first time since Friday. Your balances are there. Your money has not gone anywhere. I know what it meant to see that number on your screen, and I am glad we were able to restore that access on the timetable we set out. From tomorrow, the Bank of England and the major clearing banks will begin processing the backlog of payments that were in the system when the disruption began. Salary payments, pensions, and scheduled transfers that were authorised before last Friday will process over the coming days in the order they were submitted. We expect real-time transfers — what the industry calls Faster Payments, the system that lets you send money from your banking app — to be restored from Monday. In the meantime, the FCA has directed that no payment due between the fourteenth and twenty-first of February will be treated as late, and no fees, charges, or credit score adjustments will be applied. If your mortgage payment, your council tax, or your utility bill was due this week, it has not been missed. It has been paused.

CommunicationsInternational messaging was restored this morning. I know that many of you have been able to reach family overseas today for the first time this week. The phased restoration of internet services, email, and domestic messaging is continuing, and the National Cyber Security Centre is clearing platforms as quickly and safely as it can. These services are operating under significant congestion as several days’ worth of queued messages and emails are processed. If you have sent a message and not yet received a reply, please be patient.

SchoolsSchools reopen on Monday. ARC Education continues until then.

BusinessWe are now working with business and industry on a phased return to normal operations from Monday. Many businesses depend on cloud-hosted platforms and cannot fully reopen until those services are restored. The NCSC is prioritising the verification and restoration of business-critical cloud services over the weekend to support that reopening. A detailed sector-by-sector timetable will be published through ARC tomorrow.

We remain on track.

When I addressed you just a few short days ago, I told you that dark days were ahead. Thanks to the support and resilience of each and every one of you and the diligence of the experts safely restoring our services, those dark days are now largely behind us.

We remain on track. The great majority of services will be restored by the weekend, with normal schooling and work patterns resuming from Monday.

I will address you again tomorrow evening at six.

Be well.
